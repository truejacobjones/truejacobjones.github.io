Deposit Tracking

Product requirements

Prepared by Jacob for The Man Shop. October 4, 2026. Draft for review.

1. Summary 2. Roles 3. Where it fits 4. Requirements 5. Security and records 6. Not included 7. Open question

1. Summary

STCU does not keep or scan deposit slips. When a deposit posted by the bank does not match the POS, Accounting has no record to start from and has to trace the amount back through the stores by hand.

This feature adds a deposit log. Before a deposit goes into the safe, the employee who prepared it scans a QR code at the store, enters the date, amount, and their name, and photographs the signed slip. Accounting can then find any deposit by amount, date, store, or preparer and see the slip immediately.

The log is a reference for resolving disputed amounts. It does not feed QuickBooks or change the current QuickBooks and STCU reconciliation. It launches at the Spokane-area stores. Other stores can be turned on later from the admin app.

3. Where it fits

4. Requirements

4.1 Submitting a deposit

Each store has one QR code, posted near the safe. Scanning it with a phone camera opens a single page with the store already filled in. No app or login is needed.

FieldDetails
Business dateRequiredThe day the sales happened. Defaults to today. Can be up to 7 days in the past, never in the future.
AmountRequiredDeposit total in dollars and cents.
Your nameRequiredTyped by the employee.
Slip photoRequiredPhoto of the completed, signed slip. The page asks for the signature, store name, and date to be readable, and shows the photo for review before submitting.
NotesOptionalAnything unusual about the deposit.
  • Completing the page should take less than a minute.
  • If a deposit with the same store, date, and amount was already submitted, the page asks the employee to confirm. It does not block, since two deposits on the same night are possible.
  • The confirmation repeats the store, date, and amount, for example "Deposit submitted: North Division, 9/28/26, $1,425.00".
  • Employees cannot edit or delete a submission.

4.2 Searching and reviewing

Accounting works with deposits in a new Deposits tab on the Accounting page.

  • Filters: business date or date range, amount (exact or a range), store, and preparer name (partial match). Filters can be combined.
  • Each result shows the business date, store, amount, preparer, time submitted, and whether it has been corrected.
  • Opening a result shows the slip photo on screen next to the details.
  • The current results can be exported to CSV, which opens in Excel. Photos are not included in the export.

4.3 Corrections

  • Accounting can correct the business date, amount, preparer name, store (for example, when the wrong store's code was scanned), and notes.
  • The photo cannot be changed or replaced.
  • Every correction records who made it, when, and the old and new values. This history appears on the record, and corrected records are marked "Edited".
  • Submissions cannot be deleted.

4.4 Store view

A store login sees its own store's submissions from the last 14 days on the Online Till page, including photos, so a manager can confirm the last deposit was logged. This view is read only.

4.5 Dashboard

  • A new card shows how many stores have submitted a deposit for today, out of the stores using deposit tracking. Selecting it opens the Deposits tab.
  • The existing Store Till Status list shows a second badge for each store using deposit tracking: "Deposit Submitted" or "No Deposit".

4.6 Store setup

In the Stores tab of the Accounting page, for each store:

  • Turn deposit tracking on or off.
  • Show and print the store's QR code. It can be reprinted at any time.
  • Replace the code if it is lost or misused. The old code stops working immediately.

On the Users page, an administrator links each store login to its store. Each store has one login.

5. Security and records

  • A QR code can only add deposits for its own store. It cannot be used to view anything.
  • Viewing submissions requires signing in with the access listed in section 2.
  • Slip photos are stored privately and can only be opened by signed-in users with access.
  • Records and photos are kept indefinitely.

The typed name is for searching. The signed slip in the photo is the record of who prepared the deposit. This is why the photo is required and individual employee logins are not.

7. Open question

When Accounting corrects a deposit amount in the log, should the correction also update the cash deposit figure from the POS till report for the same store and date? That figure is what the QuickBooks integration reads.

AnswerEffect
No (recommended)The log stays separate from bookkeeping data. There is no conflict to resolve when the till report for that day is missing or differs for another reason.
YesBoth figures agree after a known correction, which may save a second cleanup in QuickBooks.

This version is built as "No". Switching to "Yes" later would add to the system without reworking it.
